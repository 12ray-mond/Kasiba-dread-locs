Why Salons & Locticians Trust Kasiba

At Kasiba Dread Locs, we understand that hair maintenance is an art form. Traditional interlocking and crotcheting techniques can cause strain on wrist joints and uneven dread density over long sessions.

Our tools are tested extensively by master locticians. Whether you're tightening Sisterlocs with our micro-latch tools or creating full instant loc sets using the powerful Max Wix machine, Kasiba guarantees snag-free operation and effortless lock structure.
